# Brand assets and usage notes / مصادر الشعارات

The portfolio displays product/tool names to describe genuine technical exposure. It does not claim endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, certification, or employment by these brands.

## Bundled vector logos

Locally bundled SVG marks (resolution independent — do not upscale raster PNGs): Docker, Python, Git, GitHub, Grafana, Prometheus, Flask, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Linux, and Grafana Loki (its 15 original polygon silhouettes, using the documented orange–yellow gradient). Brand names, shapes, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. SVG outlines were obtained from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) and [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), with appropriate brand color treatments; Docker's blue was aligned with its November 2026 published Base palette. The relative shape recognizability is the focus; this is not a substitute for an official brand-kit compliance review.

- Docker: [2026 official brand guidelines](https://www.docker.com/company/newsroom/media-resources/)
- Simple Icons: [brand outlines and trademark disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), license CC0 for icon data (individual brand marks remain trademarks)
- Devicon: [project and MIT license](https://github.com/devicons/devicon)
- Grafana: [trademark policy](https://grafana.com/trademark-policy/)

Generic competence categories like REST/API, SQL/Data, UI/UX, observability as an abstract practice, AI integration, computer vision, CI/CD as learning, and documentation use custom pictograms, *not* copied corporate brand logos. In particular, the CI/CD educational card does not imply experience with GitHub Actions.

Grafana Loki is bundled as a local vector reconstructed from the original vector polygon coordinates published in the Grafana repository (commit `04775ec940c07837bbe8a3f5fb84befb6910989a`, `loki_icon.svg`); its underlying mark belongs to Grafana Labs.

When updating: keep logo proportions unchanged; do not recolor, rotate, stretch, skew, or overlay shapes onto vendor marks. Keep the approved brand colors and adequate clear space around each vector, especially Docker (minimum height 24 px as specified in its latest guide). The containing card may have separate hover effects; the logo itself must not be animated or distorted.

The upstream Devicon MIT notice is included in [DEVICON-LICENSE.txt](assets/brand-logos/DEVICON-LICENSE.txt). The local IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Instrument Serif WOFF2 files came from the Google Fonts API. The corresponding SIL Open Font License notices are kept in [assets/fonts](assets/fonts). The standalone export includes the third-party notices.
